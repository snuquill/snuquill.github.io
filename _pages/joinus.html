JOIN US
⭐️ SNU’s one and only English press - The SNU QUILL - is looking for writers, photographers, strategic planners, and designers for its 85th edition! ⭐️

📌 Founded in 2005, SNU QUILL is the first and only English press at Seoul National University. SNU QUILL publishes once every semester and distributes around 1000 magazine copies all over the Gwanak Campus.
📌All SNU students are eligible to apply, regardless of your major, grade, or enrolled status. Exchange and graduate students are welcome as well.
📌 English proficiency is not a prerequisite as long as you are willing to learn and provide insightful ideas! (However, all content is published in English. Therefore, applicants should have a certain level of familiarity with the language)
📌 Reporters and photographers are responsible for producing articles and photographs that will be a part of the edition.
📌 Designers are responsible for editing the layout for the magazine and designing posts for SNU QUILL’s Instagram page. Though the team mainly uses Adobe InDesign and Illustrator, experience with such software is not a prerequisite: anyone interested in design is welcome to apply!
📌 The strategic planning team is responsible for developing business strategies, with each member assigned to one of three sections: External Cooperation, Marketing, or Accounting. As a member, you‘ll gain hands-on experience in areas such as planning and executing corporate collaborations, conducting digital marketing, securing funding for new ventures, and developing innovative strategies.
Past initiatives organized by the team include the English Column Contest in partnership with the Korea Herald, promotional booth activities, and writing workshops featuring journalists currently in the industry.
